    Piece guide

    ❋ Pinwheel is a giant knight: it jumps 5–7 squares in a straight line, turns 90° to either side, and goes 5–7 squares more. It leaps over everything and captures only on the square it lands on.

    ❖ Ricochet slides like a bishop and may bounce once off the board edge.

    ∿ Serpent zigzags forward or backward, one diagonal step at a time, left then right.

    ◎ Wormhole steps like a king, or teleports to its mirror square across the left–right centre if empty.

    ⊕ Archer moves to any empty square within 2 tiles (a 5×5 area), jumping over pieces. Instead of moving, it can shoot an enemy queen, king, knight or pawn in that area without leaving its square. It never captures by moving onto a piece.
